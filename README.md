## A little background

My interest in computers started when I got my first one as a kid. At 11, I managed to break one of my school's computers and had to write an apology letter to the school's IT administrator. An early introduction to incident management and communicating with a deeply unimpressed stakeholder.

In 2006, I began studying Digital Production at Portalens Gymnasium in Gothenburg.

Since then, I've worked as a VB.NET software developer, a front-end developer at web and advertising agencies, and an IT technician, before moving into automation and systems management.

I'm currently on study leave from my role as an automation engineer and systems manager to study blockchain development, deepen my programming knowledge, and explore AI.

Quite a lot has happened since I last sat in a classroom. Apparently, keeping up is now a full-time dependency.
